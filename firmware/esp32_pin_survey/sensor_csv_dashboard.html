Live readings are grouped by signal type. Analog channels show voltage plus a 0–3.3 V bar; digital pins show HIGH/LOW; touch pins show capacitance counts. GPIO labels identify where a signal is connected, not the sensor model. Press Start, choose the CSV destination, then Stop & save. On browsers with direct file access, rows are written as they arrive so long captures do not accumulate in memory.

What these readings mean

This firmware scans GPIO pins; it does not know which sensor is attached to each pin. The 67 CSV fields are 16 analog channels (voltage), 24 digital states (HIGH/LOW), and 10 touch-capacitance counts—not 67 sensors. Sensor-specific units such as °C, lux, sound level, humidity, or distance require the sensor model and its conversion/calibration. Until mapped, the analog values below are electrical output voltage only.
