# CT Dataset Catalog

This catalog is a research and engineering decision record for evaluating CT intake, geometry preservation, anatomy segmentation, and fracture labels. These are separate evaluation lanes: none of the listed cohorts establishes whole-body anatomy coverage and hairline-fracture sensitivity together.

This is not legal advice or evidence of clinical performance. Dataset permissions may not cover a commercial medical-device pipeline. Confirm terms with the data owner before acquisition or use.

Contracts reviewed: [dataset protocol](DATASET_PROTOCOL.md), [manifest schema](../../schemas/ct_dataset_manifest.schema.json), [validator](../../scripts/validate_ct_dataset.py), and [CT platform plan](PLAN.md).

## Skeletal joint validation case

[Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20305534](https://zenodo.org/records/20305534) provides 201 healthy, de-identified elbow CT cases. Orthopaedic surgeons segmented the distal humerus and proximal ulna and released CT, voxel masks, and reference STL surfaces under CC BY 4.0, as stated in the record description. The cohort does not provide a radius reference mask, fracture cases, or whole-body coverage.

One pilot case (patient 001) and its CT, mask, and humerus/ulna STL references are stored in `scratch/skeletal-primary-20261125/elbow_dataset/raw/`. This scan is 512 × 512 × 105 voxels at 0.455 × 0.455 × 1.721 mm. The CT and mask headers use reversed through-plane direction and different origins. The comparison script resamples the mask by physical coordinates onto the original CT grid; it also checks and records the model-output geometry. The CT itself is not resampled.

For this case, a CT overlay review maps reference mask label 1 to the distal humerus and label 2 to the proximal ulna. The benchmark is a cropped humeroulnar reference, not a three-bone elbow annotation: there is no radius mask. Against the exact corresponding MOOSE `clin_ct_peripheral_bones` right-side classes, the single-case Dice is 0.525 for humerus and 0.398 for ulna. Full-field HD95 is 108.3 mm and 49.8 mm respectively; interpret these cautiously because the expert masks cover joint-adjacent bone regions while MOOSE emits larger masks, including disconnected components. MOOSE emits a radius label, but it is unscored and cannot be called a validated radius from this dataset. These results show this model output is not an acceptable substitute for the source reference; they are one-case debugging metrics, not a performance estimate or clinical validation. The case is a joint-focused reference lane, not a replacement for the whole-body rendering source.

The detailed case mapping and target anatomy/status list are in [`ELBOW_ANATOMY_SCOPE.md`](ELBOW_ANATOMY_SCOPE.md). Reproduce the case metrics with [`scripts/compare_elbow_reference.py`](../../scripts/compare_elbow_reference.py) after placing the source CT, mask, and MOOSE prediction under the documented local scratch paths; the reviewed output is [`results/elbow_case001_comparison.json`](results/elbow_case001_comparison.json).

## Evaluation plan

- **Input and geometry:** Create a separately documented corpus or phantom set covering supported single-file, paired-file, multi-file, and DICOM-series adapters; varied dimensions, spacing, orientation, and field of view. Preserve original geometry and derived-volume provenance. The datasets above do not establish broad format coverage.
- **Anatomy:** Use TotalSegmentator v3 only after terms are resolved and per-scan label coverage is verified. Use VerSe for spine-specific robustness. Report per-structure denominators and exclusions.
- Use the downloaded BoneHub male subject as a local whole-body rendering/integration case only. Its two-subject source and grouped/incomplete labels do not establish general anatomy or fracture performance.
- Use the healthy elbow cases as a separate joint-focused validation lane; they cannot establish whole-body skeleton coverage, radius segmentation, fracture performance, or pathology robustness.
- **Fractures:** Keep RibFrac and RSNA C-spine as distinct region-specific challenge lanes. Report each label set's sampling design and limits.
- **Natural prevalence:** None of these entries is established here as a consecutive or probability-sampled cohort. Curate a separate cohort retaining natural prevalence before estimating prevalence or predictive values.
- **Hairline/subtle fractures:** Curate a separate expert-reviewed, adjudicated challenge cohort with a calibrated subtlety rubric, acquisition details, and reviewed negatives. None of these public sources proves hairline sensitivity or replaces prospective expert review.

## Acquisition checklist

1. **Terms/access:** Confirm exact release, research and commercial permissions, attribution, share-alike obligations, registration, redistribution limits, and permitted model-training/evaluation uses. Track these in this decision record or acquisition documentation; the manifest schema has no license field.
2. **Privacy:** Verify de-identification and minimize source metadata. Use opaque pseudonyms. Do not store names, exact dates, accessions, DICOM UIDs, facility names, or re-identification mappings in the repository. .gitignore does not de-identify or secure files.
3. **Identity/splits:** Audit patient and site overlap across releases and cohorts. Keep a patient group within one split. For site-held-out, reserve whole sites for external_test and verify exclusivity; release names alone do not prove this.
4. **Integrity/paths:** Verify package checksums outside the manifest if needed for acquisition operations. Store source paths in source.paths relative to the data root and confirm validator containment.
5. **Adapters/geometry:** Record source.kind, source.format_id, and source.adapter_id. Verify dimensions, spacing, origin, direction, coordinate frame, intensity scaling, and warnings. Preserve original_geometry; record each conversion/resampling in derived_volumes with separate geometry and conversion history.
6. **Sampling:** Use cohort_ids to distinguish geometry, anatomy, enriched challenge, and prevalence roles. Do not estimate natural prevalence or uncorrected PPV from enriched challenge data.
7. **Coverage/status:** Use region_fov for scan coverage. In anatomy_annotations keep fov_status separate from annotation_status (annotated, not_annotated, uncertain, not_applicable). Missing annotation is unknown, never a negative. Keep reference review_status distinct from model_runs processing status.
8. **Fracture review:** Populate fracture_assessment and its review object explicitly. Negative requires complete reviewed coverage; positive requires fracture_lesions. Record only supported localization and review/adjudication details.
9. **Extensions:** Use extensions only for pre-reviewed, non-identifying x- fields allowed by the schema. Do not add source_path, extra_metadata, or license_confirmed to manifest records without a separately reviewed schema revision.

The BoneHub male CT and reference segmentations were downloaded to the user's Desktop and used for the local rendering checkpoint; they were not copied into the repository as dataset records. No model weights were downloaded. Do not treat this two-subject integration example as a benchmark or report clinical performance from it.
