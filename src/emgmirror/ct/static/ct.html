Understand your CT scan

Load the scan your hospital gave you. This page turns it into a 3D model you can rotate, with the bones, muscle and fat measured in millimetres and explained in plain words.

Your scan stays on this computer. Nothing is uploaded to the internet.

1. Choose your scan

Drag the scan folder or a zip file here

A CT scan is usually a folder with hundreds of files, often on a CD or USB stick, sometimes named DICOM or IMAGES.

The scan is already in a folder on this computer

Paste the folder's location. This is faster for large scans because nothing is copied.

2. Building your model

Starting

A full-resolution scan can take a long time on a small laptop. You can leave this page open.

3. Your scan

Look around

Drag to rotate. Tick boxes to show or hide parts. Use the sliders to move through the scan slice by slice.
